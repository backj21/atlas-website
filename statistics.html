About The Team

A cross-disciplinary team focused on practical data analytics.

Our Statistics team includes students from diverse majors, including Statistics, IS+DS, and Marketing, who all share the goal of developing strong data analytical skills. We build hands-on projects throughout the year and also participate in the annual datathon hosted by the University of Illinois Statistics Department.
